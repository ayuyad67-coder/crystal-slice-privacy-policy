breetex ("we", "our", or "us") built the Crystal Slices app as a free-to-play mobile game with optional in-app purchases and advertisements. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our game.

1. Information We Collect

To provide an engaging gaming experience, save your progress across devices, process purchases, and display ads, we collect and process the following information:

  • Account Information: When you sign in with Google or use Guest login via Firebase Authentication, we collect your unique Firebase User ID (UID), display name (if applicable), and email address.
  • Game Progress & Statistics: We store your highest unlocked level (1 to 2000+), Endless mode scores, stage completion status, virtual currency balances (coins), and unlocked knife skins.
  • In-App Purchases: Purchase transactions (such as coin packs and the permanent Remove Ads pass) are processed securely through Google Play Billing. We store purchase verification tokens and active entitlement statuses; we never collect or store your credit card or payment credentials.
  • Advertising Data: We utilize Google AdMob to serve optional rewarded advertisements and interstitials. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as Advertising ID) to deliver relevant ads in accordance with your device and Google ad settings.

2. Use of Firebase Services

Crystal Slices integrates Firebase services provided by Google:

  • Firebase Authentication: Manages secure user sign-in and guest profile session restoration.
  • Firebase Firestore: Stores your cloud game saves and profile preferences securely under strict security rules.

3. Google AdMob & Advertisements

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may use device identifiers, IP addresses, and interaction data to serve contextual or personalized advertisements. You can manage your ad personalization settings in your Google account or device settings.

4. Google Play Billing

All real-money purchases within the game are handled exclusively by Google Play Billing. Google shares transaction confirmation tokens with our app to unlock your purchased items (coins or ad-free status).

5. Data Security & Retention

We implement robust administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your data. Your game progress and account information are retained as long as your account remains active.

6. Account Deletion & Data Control

You have full control over your data. You can permanently delete your account, game progress, cloud saves, and associated profile data at any time directly within the game via the "Delete Account & Cloud Progress" button in the settings menu.

7. Children's Privacy

Crystal Slices does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can promptly remove it.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
